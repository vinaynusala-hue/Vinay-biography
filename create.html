🌐 How To Develop A Website

Website development is the process of converting an idea into a working website.

💡 What is Website Development?

Website development means creating and maintaining websites that people can access through a web browser.

A developer normally starts with an idea, plans the website structure, creates the design, writes the code, tests everything and finally publishes the website online.

Websites can be simple personal pages, portfolios, business websites, blogs, educational platforms or large web applications.
